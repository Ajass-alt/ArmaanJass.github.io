Autonomous Quadrotor Platform
UAV / Flight-tested

Ground-up carbon-fiber quadrotor airframe designed for payload delivery in constrained environments. Owned structural design, propulsion sizing, and mechanical integration of flight controller, ESCs, and companion computer. Iterated through three airframe revisions to cut structural weight by 22% while holding stiffness targets under FEA.

Terrain-Adaptive Rover Suspension
Ground robotics

Independent rocker-bogie derived suspension for a 6-wheel research rover, designed to maintain wheel contact across uneven terrain without active control. Full kinematic simulation in MATLAB preceded physical prototyping; final geometry validated on a custom test rig with 15cm obstacle clearance.

Obstacle clearance150 mm
