About

If I could put into words the feeling I get when I run into a problem I can't solve, I probably wouldn't be an engineer. But when the solution finally clicks, it's right up there with bombing a drive down the fairway or scoring a goal in front of the people you love.

My recent work has centred on designing, simulating and fabricating parts for drones and cars. Alongside that, I've picked up hands-on experience troubleshooting and installing systems as an irrigation technician — plenty of exposure to real mechanical, electrical and hydraulic systems outside of a CAD screen.

My interest in engineering traces back to the first time I watched Iron Man — since then I've chased the same loop: learn something, apply it, then go learn more.
